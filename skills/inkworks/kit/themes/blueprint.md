## Overview

A cyanotype print: the paper is the dark navy of a blueprint and the ink is near-white. Everything else follows the drafting rules, inverted.

## Colors

On dark paper the 3D materials sit darker than the ink, so the white outlines carry the shapes; a material lighter than about `#5a88c4` washes into the ink. Hues are brightened so they hold on navy. Glass and ghost fills are pale and very thin.

## Typography

Archivo Narrow for the headline, Archivo for body text, JetBrains Mono for numbers: a stencil-office feel rather than a serif one.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the paper flat navy. No gradients, no vignette.
- Don't use pure white for the ink; `#eaf2ff` keeps the print feel.
