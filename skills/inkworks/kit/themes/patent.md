## Overview

The look of a patent office drawing: warm ivory stock, brown-black ink, engraved type and typewritten figures. The cream ground is the brief, not a default.

## Colors

Muted, period hues: a slate blue, a mustard, an oxide red and a dun grey. Materials are pale parchment tones so the ink outline does the drawing, as an engraving would.

## Typography

IM Fell English for the headline and phase names, Libre Caslon Text for body text, Courier Prime for readouts and the chart. Neither family has a 500 or 600 weight, so the theme loads 400 and 700 only.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the ink one colour. Period drawings had no tints.
- Don't add paper texture or stains; the flat ivory already reads as old stock.
